Common Questions

FAQ

Full transparency — no hidden costs, no vague timelines. Here's what clients ask most before working with us.

Can't find your answer? We respond fast.

Ask on WhatsApp
How long does a website take to build?
Typical business website: 2–4 weeks. Complex projects with custom features, e-commerce, or multiple pages: 4–8 weeks. We always give a clear timeline upfront — no surprises.
What is your pricing? Do you have packages?
Every project is scoped individually. Websites start from ₹15,000, video editing from ₹5,000, app development from ₹50,000, AI agents from ₹20,000, and full digital packages from ₹30,000. Book a free call for a detailed proposal.
Do you build mobile apps and AI systems?
Yes. We build native and cross-platform apps for iOS and Android, custom AI agents, chatbots, automation workflows, and full management software (ERP/CRM). These are core services at STACK-UP.
Do you work with clients outside Dehradun / Greater Noida?
Absolutely. We work with clients across India and internationally via WhatsApp, email, and video calls. Many of our happiest clients have never met us in person.
What do I need to provide to get started?
Just your idea and goals — we handle the rest. For websites: branding, existing content, and reference sites. For apps: concept and feature list. For AI: your business workflow. We guide you through everything with a clear onboarding checklist.
How many revisions are included?
All projects include 2–3 revision rounds at each stage. We work in close collaboration, so by final delivery you'll already love what we've built. Additional revisions beyond included rounds are charged at a fair hourly rate.
Will my website / app be mobile-friendly?
Always. Everything we build is fully responsive — designed and tested on phones, tablets, and all screen sizes. In India, 80%+ of web traffic is mobile. We build mobile-first by default.
Do you offer ongoing support after launch?
Yes. We offer post-launch support packages for maintenance, updates, performance monitoring, and feature additions. Many clients stay with us for months or years after their initial project.
