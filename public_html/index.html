FAQ

Straight answers. Anything else, ask Teddy or talk to us.

01Where do we start?

With a free intro call. Tell us the problem, we'll tell you honestly whether we're the right fit and what it would take.

02How is pricing set?

By scope, written down before work begins. You approve a fixed proposal, so the number never moves without your say.

03Can you take over an existing product?

Yes. We audit what's there, keep what works and rebuild only what holds you back.

04Do you work with teams abroad?

All the time. We plan overlap hours, share a live board and demo over video every week.

05Who owns the code and designs?

You do. Source files, repositories and accounts are handed over in full at launch.

06What happens after launch?

Pick a care plan for updates and monitoring, or take it in-house with our documentation. Either way, we're a message away.
