All pricing is communicated to the Client before work begins. The following payment structure applies:

  • Advance Payment: A non-refundable advance (typically 50%–100% of the total project cost) is required before any work commences. The exact advance amount will be specified in the Project Agreement.
  • Milestone Payments: For larger projects, payments may be split across agreed milestones. Each milestone payment is due upon reaching the defined deliverable stage.
  • Final Payment: The remaining balance is due upon project completion before final files or access credentials are delivered to the Client.
  • Accepted Payment Methods: Bank transfer, UPI, PayPal, Wise, or other methods agreed upon in writing.

Final deliverables, source files, login credentials, and project assets will only be transferred to the Client after all outstanding payments have been received in full.

Revisions & Changes

Each project includes a defined number of revision rounds as specified in the Project Agreement. Revisions are defined as minor adjustments within the agreed scope — not fundamental changes to the direction, concept, or scope of work.

  • Revision requests must be submitted in writing (email or WhatsApp) within 7 days of delivery.
  • Revisions beyond the agreed number will be charged at an hourly rate communicated in advance.
  • Requests to change the project direction, concept, or core scope after work has begun are considered new work and will be quoted separately.
  • Approval of a design or deliverable (verbally, in writing, or by silence within 7 days) is considered final acceptance.
Clients are encouraged to provide clear, consolidated feedback in each round to ensure efficient revisions. Scattered or contradictory feedback does not entitle the Client to additional revision rounds.
